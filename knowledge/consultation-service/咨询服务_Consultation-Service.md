# 咨询服务 / Consultation Services

## 🎯 服务概述 / Service Overview

基于"玄学是科学吗？"的哲学框架，我们提供专业的咨询服务，帮助个人和组织在不确定情境下做出更明智的决策。
Based on the philosophical framework of "Is Fortune-Telling Science?", we provide professional consultation services to help individuals and organizations make wiser decisions in uncertain situations.

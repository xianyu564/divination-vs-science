## 🌟 理念说明 / Philosophical Statement

### 理想主义愿景 / Idealistic Vision

我们相信，在科技迸发的时代，传统的命理学和国学智慧正迎来前所未有的革新机遇。通过将古老的东方智慧与现代科技思维相结合，我们可以为人类决策和认知提供新的可能性。

**This repository represents our idealistic vision**: In this era of technological explosion, traditional numerology and Chinese classical wisdom are encountering unprecedented opportunities for innovation. By combining ancient Eastern wisdom with modern technological thinking, we can provide new possibilities for human decision-making and cognition.

### 开源共享的哲学 / Philosophy of Open Source Sharing

本目录的部分开源内容属于开发者践行"知识普惠"理想的一部分。我们相信，真正的智慧应该超越商业利益的束缚，为全人类所共享。通过GitHub这样的开放平台，我们能够：

- 展示哲学框架的完整性和系统性
- 让更多人了解传统智慧与现代科学的结合可能
- 维护代码仓库的完整性和云端可持续性
- 促进学术交流和思想碰撞

**The partial open-sourcing of content in this directory is part of the developer's ideal of "knowledge accessibility for all."** We believe that true wisdom should transcend commercial interests and be shared by all humanity. Through open platforms like GitHub, we can:

- Demonstrate the completeness and systematic nature of the philosophical framework
- Help more people understand the possibilities of combining traditional wisdom with modern science
- Maintain the integrity and cloud sustainability of the code repository
- Promote academic exchange and intellectual collision
